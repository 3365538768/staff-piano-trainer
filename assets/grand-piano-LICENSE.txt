This application bundles 25 existing MP3 recordings, C2 through C8 in minor-third
steps, from that distribution. The downloaded MP3 bytes are unchanged. They are
base64 encoded into grand-piano.js so double-clicked HTML can play them offline
without file-origin fetch restrictions. Each sample includes its original file
name, byte length, and SHA-256 digest. Playback shifts the nearest recording to
the requested note and applies only volume, a short click-free attack, and
key-release gain control. No additional synthesized tone or reverb is mixed in.
This is a single-velocity MP3 selection, not the complete 16-layer instrument.
